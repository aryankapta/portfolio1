About Me

THE MAN BEHIND
THE BAR.

A five-year journey from a hospitality classroom in Shimla to the bar of a 5-star property, built one shift, one shake, and one signature drink at a time.

My professional journey began in 2020 at IBTHM Shimla, where I trained in the fundamentals of professional bartending. I sharpened those fundamentals on the floor at The Finch, Chandigarh, as an Assistant Bartender. My first real bar.

In late 2021, I moved to Goa and joined Romeo Lane, Vagator, as an Assistant Bartender, earning a promotion to Bartender over the course of a year behind the bar. From there, I stepped into Thalassa, The Greek Tavern, the sunset-view property in Goa, where I spent another year refining my craft.

Most recently, I relocated to Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, for a stint at a five-star property, where I levelled up across volume, service standards, and signature menu work. All of which I now bring to my current role in Dubai.

Every cocktail tells a story. My job is to make sure the guest remembers theirs.
